Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Noorani Qaida is an educational Islamic application developed by Strivers. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information Collection

You can use Noorani Qaida without creating an account or providing personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

If you choose to submit feedback through the app, the following information may be collected:

  • Feedback category
  • Feedback message
  • Anonymous Firebase User ID
  • App version
  • Android version
  • Device model
  • Device language
  • Country or region (if available)
  • Feedback status
  • Date and time of submission

This information is collected only to help improve the app, investigate reported issues, and provide a better user experience.

Feedback

Submitting feedback is completely optional.

Feedback is securely stored using Google Firebase services to help improve the app, fix issues, and respond to user feedback.

Users may remove feedback from their own feedback list within the app.

Advertisements

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process certain information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Noorani Qaida is designed for educational purposes and may be used by children with parental guidance. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase Authentication (Anonymous Sign-In)
  • Google Firebase Firestore
  • Google Play Services

These services may collect technical information necessary to provide their functionality in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated revision date.
