Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PanjSurah is an Islamic application developed by Strivers. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may use, how it is used, and your choices.

Information Collection

You can use PanjSurah without creating an account or providing personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Depending on the features you use, PanjSurah may process information such as:

  • Device location, when you choose to use current location
  • Selected city or location
  • Latitude and longitude required for prayer time calculations
  • Prayer and notification preferences
  • Recent locations
  • Tasbeeh and other app preferences stored on your device

This information is used only as necessary to provide the app's features and improve your experience.

Location

PanjSurah may request access to your device location to calculate prayer times and provide location-based prayer information.

Location access is optional. You can manually search for or select a city instead of allowing access to your device location.

When you search for a location, the search query and selected location information may be processed by an online location service to find matching cities, towns, or other locations.

Prayer Times

PanjSurah uses selected location information, such as a city or geographic coordinates, to obtain and calculate prayer times and related Islamic timings.

Location information necessary for these features may be sent to third-party prayer time or location services.

Recent Locations and App Preferences

PanjSurah may store recent locations and app preferences locally on your device to make the app easier to use.

This may include your selected location, prayer settings, notification settings, reading preferences, Tasbeeh information, and other app preferences.

Notifications and Alarms

PanjSurah may use Android notification and alarm features to provide Azaan, prayer time, Jummah, and other reminders that you choose to enable.

Depending on your Android version, the app may request notification or alarm-related permissions required to provide these features at the correct time.

Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted third-party services to provide certain functionality, including:

  • Prayer time services
  • Location and geocoding services
  • Google Play Services

These services may process limited technical or location information necessary to provide their functionality in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

PanjSurah provides Islamic and religious content that may be used by people of different ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information is only shared with service providers when necessary to provide app functionality or when required by law.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated revision date.
