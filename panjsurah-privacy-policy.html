PanjSurah Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

PanjSurah is developed and provided by Strivers2026. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PanjSurah mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

PanjSurah is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. The app does not require users to create an account for normal use.

Depending on the features you use, the app may process location information, app preferences, notification settings, and locally stored data needed to provide its features.

2. Location Information

PanjSurah may request access to your device location in order to calculate prayer times and provide location-based prayer information.

You may also manually select or search for a city or location instead of using your device's current location.

Location information is used only for app features that require location, such as prayer time calculations.

3. Manual Location Search

When you search for a city, town, or other location, the app may send the search text to an external geocoding service in order to return matching locations.

The selected location may include information such as city name, country, latitude, and longitude so that the app can calculate prayer times accurately.

4. Recent Locations

PanjSurah may store a small number of recently selected locations on your device so that you can quickly select them again.

Recent locations are stored locally on your device and are not intended to identify you personally.

5. Prayer Time Services

PanjSurah may communicate with third-party online services to retrieve or calculate prayer times, Ramadan timings, location information, or related Islamic calendar data.

These services may receive technical request information such as your selected city or geographic coordinates when necessary to provide the requested information.

6. Notifications and Alarms

PanjSurah may use Android notification and alarm features to provide prayer, Azaan, Jummah, reminder, and other Islamic notifications that you choose to enable.

Depending on your Android version, the app may request notification permission or access to Android's alarms and reminders settings so notifications can be delivered at the appropriate time.

7. Local App Data

PanjSurah may store app settings and preferences locally on your device, including selected location, prayer preferences, Tasbeeh data, reading preferences, notification settings, and recent locations.

This local data is used to provide app functionality and remember your preferences.

8. Internet Access

PanjSurah uses an internet connection for certain features, including obtaining prayer times, searching locations, retrieving online data, and other features that depend on external services.

9. Third-Party Services

PanjSurah may use third-party services or libraries to provide certain features. These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Services used by PanjSurah may include prayer-time APIs, geocoding/location services, and Google or Firebase services where applicable.

10. Firebase Services

Some PanjSurah features may use Google Firebase services for functionality such as backup, synchronization, analytics, crash reporting, or other app services when enabled or included in the application.

Google may process technical device or app information according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

11. Advertising

PanjSurah may include advertising in current or future versions of the application. If advertising services are used, those providers may process limited device, advertising, or technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Any advertising implementation will be handled in accordance with applicable Google Play policies and advertising requirements.

12. Children's Privacy

PanjSurah contains Islamic educational and religious content that may also be suitable for families and younger users.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly through the app.

13. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may only be shared with service providers when required to provide a feature requested by the user, comply with legal requirements, protect users, or maintain the security and operation of the application.

14. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary data collection and protect information used by PanjSurah.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Your Choices

You can control permissions such as location and notifications through your Android device settings.

You may also choose manual location selection instead of granting location access, where supported by the app.

16. Data Retention

Locally stored app settings and recent locations generally remain on your device until they are replaced, reset, cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Data handled by third-party services may be retained according to the policies of those service providers.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PanjSurah features, services, legal requirements, or Google Play policies change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

18. Contact

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or PanjSurah, please contact the developer using the support contact information provided on the PanjSurah Google Play Store listing.
