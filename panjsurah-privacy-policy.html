Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

PanjSurah is an Islamic application developed by Strivers. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app and its service providers may process, how it is used, and your available choices.

1. Information Collection

You can use PanjSurah without providing your name, email address, or phone number.

Depending on the features you use, PanjSurah and its service providers may process:

  • Device location, when permission is granted
  • Manually selected city or location
  • Geographic coordinates for prayer calculations
  • Prayer and notification preferences
  • Recent locations
  • Reading progress and bookmarks
  • Tasbeeh counts and app preferences
  • Device identifiers and technical information
  • App usage and diagnostic information

2. Location Information

PanjSurah may request device location permission to provide accurate prayer times and other location-based Islamic information.

Location permission is optional. You can manually search for or select a city instead of using your current location.

Location searches and geographic coordinates may be processed by third-party location or prayer time services when necessary.

3. Prayer Times

PanjSurah uses selected location information, prayer calculation methods, and geographic coordinates to calculate or retrieve prayer times and related Islamic timings.

Information necessary for these features may be sent to third-party prayer time or location services.

4. Local Storage and Preferences

PanjSurah may store certain information locally on your device to improve usability.

This may include:

  • Selected and recent locations
  • Prayer calculation settings
  • Notification preferences
  • Reading positions and bookmarks
  • Tasbeeh counts and preferences
  • Other application settings

5. Notifications and Alarms

PanjSurah may use Android notification and alarm features to provide Azaan, prayer reminders, Jummah reminders, and other enabled notifications.

Depending on your Android version, the app may request notification and alarm-related permissions.

You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

6. Advertising - Google AdMob

PanjSurah uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and provide advertising services.

This information may include:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Advertising and device identifiers
  • Ad interactions
  • Device and diagnostic information

Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable requirements, user consent, and settings.

For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy .

7. Firebase Services

PanjSurah uses Google Firebase services for supported application functionality, settings, and analytics.

Firebase Analytics may collect app usage events, device characteristics, app-instance identifiers, and diagnostic information to help improve the application.

Firebase Authentication may create anonymous identifiers when authentication is used by an enabled app feature.

Firebase Cloud Firestore may process configuration information and other data necessary for enabled application features.

For more information, visit Firebase Privacy and Security .

8. Third-Party Services

PanjSurah may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase
  • Google Play Services
  • Prayer time services
  • Location and geocoding services

These services may process limited information necessary to provide their functionality in accordance with their applicable privacy policies.

9. Data Sharing

Strivers does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers for app functionality, advertising, analytics, security, or when required by law.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application information can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling PanjSurah.

This does not necessarily remove information already processed by third-party services.

To request deletion of applicable information held by Strivers, contact us at: strivers.helpdesk@gmail.com .

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention obligations.

11. Children's Privacy

PanjSurah provides Islamic and religious content that may be useful to people of different ages.

We do not intentionally request children's names or contact information to access the app's main religious content.

Third-party advertising and analytics services may process technical information. Additional privacy protections may apply to children depending on applicable laws and the app's audience settings.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through PanjSurah.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Your Choices

You can:

  • Use manual city selection instead of GPS
  • Manage location permissions
  • Manage notifications and alarms
  • Manage available advertising privacy settings
  • Clear locally stored application data
  • Contact us regarding applicable data requests

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
