Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

PanjSurah is an Islamic application developed by Strivers. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information PanjSurah and its service providers may collect or process, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect and Use

You can use the main features of PanjSurah without providing your name, email address, or phone number. Some features and integrated services may process information automatically or when you choose to use them.

Depending on your use of the app, this may include:

  • Device location or manually selected city
  • Geographic coordinates for prayer calculations
  • Prayer time and notification preferences
  • Recently selected locations
  • Reading positions, bookmarks and Tasbeeh data
  • Device identifiers and technical information
  • App usage and diagnostic information
  • Messages you voluntarily send through support features
  • Anonymous authentication identifiers, where applicable

2. Location Information

PanjSurah may request access to your device location to provide prayer times and other location-based features. Location access is optional.

You may manually select or search for a city instead of granting location permission.

Location searches, selected cities, or geographic coordinates may be processed by third-party location, geocoding, or prayer time services when necessary to provide the requested information.

3. Prayer Times and Reminders

PanjSurah uses your selected location and prayer calculation preferences to calculate or retrieve prayer times and related Islamic timings.

The app may request notification and alarm permissions to provide Azaan, prayer reminders, Jummah reminders, and other notifications you choose to enable.

You can manage these permissions through your device's Android settings.

4. Local App Storage

PanjSurah may store information locally on your device to remember your preferences and improve your experience.

This may include:

  • Selected and recent locations
  • Prayer calculation settings
  • Notification preferences
  • Reading progress and bookmarks
  • Tasbeeh counts and preferences
  • Other app settings

Locally stored data may be removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the application.

5. Advertising — Google AdMob

PanjSurah uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information to deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud, and support advertising services.

This information may include:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers
  • Ad interactions and app usage information
  • Device information and diagnostic data

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized, depending on applicable requirements, consent, device settings, and advertising configuration.

For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Firebase and App Analytics

PanjSurah uses Google Firebase services for supported app functionality, remote configuration, authentication, and analytics.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as app interactions, usage events, device characteristics, approximate location, and app-instance identifiers to help us understand and improve the application.

Firebase Authentication may generate an anonymous user identifier when anonymous authentication is used for a supported feature.

Firebase Cloud Firestore may store information necessary for features such as remote configuration, support messages, and related app functionality.

These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy and data processing terms.

7. Support and Feedback

If you contact Strivers by email or through an in-app support feature, we may process the information you voluntarily provide, including your message, contact details if supplied, and technical information needed to respond or investigate an issue.

Support messages may be stored using Firebase services where the in-app messaging feature is enabled.

8. Third-Party Services

PanjSurah may use third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase
  • Google Play Services
  • Prayer time services
  • Location and geocoding services

These services may process information necessary for their functions in accordance with their applicable privacy policies.

9. Data Sharing

Strivers does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers for app functionality, advertising, analytics, security, technical support, or when required by law.

Advertising providers may independently process certain information as described in their own privacy policies.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app information can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. This does not necessarily delete information previously processed or stored by online service providers.

To request deletion of applicable information held by Strivers, including support messages where available, please contact: strivers.helpdesk@gmail.com.

Please identify PanjSurah and describe the data you want deleted. We may request information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant records. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention obligations.

11. Children's Privacy

PanjSurah provides Islamic and educational content that may be useful to people of different ages. We do not intentionally request children's names or contact information for access to the app's main religious content.

Because third-party advertising and analytics services may process device information, additional privacy protections may apply to children depending on their age, region, and applicable laws.

Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning a child.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app. Third-party services may also apply their own security measures.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Your Choices

You can:

  • Use manual location selection instead of GPS
  • Manage location and notification permissions
  • Manage available advertising privacy controls
  • Clear locally stored app data
  • Contact us about applicable data deletion requests

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
